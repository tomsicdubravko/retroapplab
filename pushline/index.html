HOW TO PLAY

01 · PUSH

Pick a movable block and drag it left or right into empty space. Release to finish your move. On desktop, you can also select a block and use the arrow keys. Blocks fall when the space below their whole width is empty.

02 · ALIGN & CLEAR

Fill all 8 cells of a row to clear it. Complete several rows at once for a combo and more points. Falling blocks can create more clears. A perfect clear after clearing rows earns 3,000 bonus points.

03 · WATCH THE NEXT ROW

NEXT ROW previews the row added after your completed move. Empty or locked boards add rows automatically. New rows appear above the highest occupied row, then fall with gravity.

04 · KEEP ROOM AT THE TOP

The red line warns when the board is getting high. The game ends when a new row must be added and the top row is occupied. Levels increase every 500 points and unlock special blocks.
